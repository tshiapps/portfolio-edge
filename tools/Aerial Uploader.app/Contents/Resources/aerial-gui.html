Flickr won't hand full-size images to the website's servers, so this downloads them on your connection, resizes them here on your Mac (same sizes and quality as the site — panoramas included, no file-size limit) and uploads the finished files. Review Instagram ↗Review Getty ↗

Finds which Instagram post shows which photo on the site — even when Instagram's version is cropped to 4:5 or square, filtered or re-edited. Everything runs on this Mac (best on the Mac Studio): an image model (DINOv2) shortlists look-alikes, then matching details are lined up to confirm. Clear matches are linked automatically; near-duplicates go to admin → Instagram for you to pick. The first run installs the matcher (~1.5 GB, once) and reads every site photo; after that it only looks at new posts. Getty works the same way on your Getty portfolio (import DeepMeta's export in admin → Getty first): it fetches each asset's watermarked preview once and ignores the watermark; assets already linked by file name or by you are skipped.
